Comprehensive privacy notice

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This is an English translation provided for convenience. The Spanish version is the legally binding text under Mexican law.

In compliance with Mexico’s Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (the “Law”), we provide this Privacy Notice to explain which personal data we process, why, and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Data controller

Aaron Norberto Gutierrez González, an individual engaged in business and professional activities under the trade name The Key Audit (“The Key Audit”), with an address for notices at Av. Punto Sur 312, Punto sur, 45640, Tlajomulco de Zuniga, Jalisco, Mexico, is responsible for processing your personal data.

Privacy contact: contacto@thekeyaudit.com

2. Personal data we process

Depending on your relationship with us, we may process the following categories of data:

If you are…Data we may process
A website visitorTechnical and browsing data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, visit duration and interactions, collected through analytics cookies.
A prospect (form, email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn)Identification: name. Contact: email address and phone number. Professional: company and job title. The content of the messages you send us.
A client or a client’s representativeAll of the above, plus tax and billing data: RFC (tax ID), legal name, tax regime, tax address, CFDI use and email for invoices. Where applicable, legal representation data.

Sensitive personal data. We do not request sensitive personal data (for example, health, ethnic origin, beliefs or union membership) through the website, forms or contact channels. Please do not include it in your messages.

Financial or asset data. We do not request card or bank account details through the website.

Minors. Our services are aimed at companies and professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

3. Purposes of processing

a) Primary purposes (these do not require your consent, as they are necessary to respond to your request or to the service relationship):

  • Responding to and following up on your information requests.
  • Preparing and sending professional service proposals and quotes.
  • Scheduling and holding work meetings.
  • Entering into, performing and managing the professional services agreement.
  • Issuing tax invoices (CFDI), managing collections and meeting tax and legal obligations.
  • Keeping a record of communications with prospects and clients.
  • Operating the website, keeping it secure and measuring its performance statistically.

b) Secondary purposes (these require your consent):

  • Sending you newsletters, articles, invitations to webinars or events, and content about internal audit, internal control and GRC.
  • Sending you commercial information about new services.
  • Conducting satisfaction surveys.

If you do not want your data used for secondary purposes, you can say so now by leaving the corresponding box in the form unchecked, or at any time by writing to contacto@thekeyaudit.com with the subject “Baja de comunicaciones” (unsubscribe). You can also unsubscribe through the link included in each email. Declining will not affect how we handle your request or provide our services.

4. Data our clients share with us during engagements

In internal audit, internal control, compliance or consulting engagements, our clients may give us access to information containing personal data of their employees, suppliers or customers. For that information, The Key Audit acts as a data processor on behalf of the client, who is the data controller.

That data is processed solely to perform the contracted service, following the client’s instructions and the terms of the agreement and confidentiality agreements. It is not used for our own purposes and is returned or destroyed when the engagement ends, unless there is a legal or professional obligation to keep working papers.

5. Cookies and tracking technologies

Our website uses Google Analytics 4, which sets analytics cookies to produce visit statistics (pages viewed, traffic source, device and approximate location). We do not use this information to identify you personally.

We also use Google Search Console to understand how the website appears in search results; this tool does not set cookies in your browser.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. If you disable them, the website will keep working. More information is available in Google’s privacy policy.

6. Service providers and transfers

The Key Audit does not sell or rent your personal data.

To operate, we rely on providers that process data on our behalf (processors), such as web hosting, email, cloud storage, messaging (WhatsApp) and analytics (Google). Some of them may store information on servers located outside Mexico, under their own security measures.

We do not make transfers of personal data that require your consent. We may disclose your data without consent only in the cases permitted by the Law, for example: to the Tax Administration Service (SAT) for tax purposes, to competent authorities that request it on a duly founded and reasoned basis, or when necessary to establish, exercise or defend a right in legal proceedings.

If in the future we need to transfer your data to a third party in any other case, we will update this notice and ask for your consent.

7. ARCO rights and objection to automated decisions

You have the right to:

  • Access: know what data we hold and how we use it.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Cancellation: ask us to delete your data where applicable.
  • Objection: object to processing for specific purposes, including automated processing that affects you without human involvement. The Key Audit does not make automated decisions about individuals.

How to exercise them. Send an email to contacto@thekeyaudit.com with the subject “Derechos ARCO” (ARCO rights), including:

  • Your name and how you would like to receive our reply.
  • A copy of an official ID or, if acting through a representative, the document proving the representation.
  • A clear description of the data and the right you wish to exercise.
  • Any information that helps us locate your data. For rectification, state the correction and attach supporting evidence.

Timeframes. We will respond within 20 business days of receiving your request. If it is granted, we will carry it out within the following 15 business days. Both periods may be extended once for an equal period when circumstances justify it, and we will let you know.

Cancellation results in a blocking period before final deletion, as required by the Law. We cannot cancel data we must keep due to a legal obligation, for example, a tax obligation.

8. Withdrawing consent and limiting use

You can withdraw your consent or limit the use or disclosure of your data at any time by writing to contacto@thekeyaudit.com with the subject “Revocación” (withdrawal) or “Limitación” (limitation), stating the data or purposes it refers to. We will add you to our internal exclusion list. Withdrawal is not retroactive and does not apply to processing needed to meet current legal or contractual obligations.

9. Security and confidentiality measures

We apply administrative, technical and physical measures to protect your data against damage, loss, alteration, destruction, or unauthorized use, access or processing: access control, two-step authentication, encryption in transit and confidentiality agreements with everyone involved in processing. The duty of confidentiality continues even after the relationship with The Key Audit ends.

If a security breach significantly affects your rights, we will inform you without delay so you can take action.

10. Data retention

  • Prospects: up to 2 years from the last contact, unless it becomes a service relationship.
  • Clients: for the duration of the service relationship and 5 years afterward, the retention period for accounting and tax records.
  • Browsing data: according to the retention period set in Google Analytics: 12 months.

Once these periods end, the data is blocked and then deleted.

11. Authority

If you believe your right to personal data protection has been violated, you may contact the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (Ministry of Anti-Corruption and Good Governance), the competent authority for personal data held by private parties in Mexico.

12. Changes to this Privacy Notice

We may update this notice due to legal changes, or changes to our services or tools. We will publish the current version, with its update date, at https://thekeyaudit.com/aviso-de-privacidad (Spanish) and https://thekeyaudit.com/en/privacy-notice (English). If a change involves new purposes that require your consent, we will ask for it by email.
